Section 3

Information Automatically Collected by Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services that automatically collect certain information about your device and how you use the App. We do not control what these services collect; we use the information they make available to us to operate, improve, and monetize the App.

3.1 Google Play Services

Provided by Google LLC. Used to ensure compatibility with Android devices and to authenticate that the App is genuine.

3.2 Google Analytics for Firebase

Provided by Google LLC. Collects anonymized data about how users interact with the App, including:

  • Device type, model, and operating system version
  • App version
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address — country/region level, not GPS)
  • Aggregated, anonymized usage events (such as which screens are viewed and which features are used)
  • A randomly generated installation identifier ("Firebase Installation ID")

This information helps us understand how the App is used and improve its features. It is not linked to your personal identity.

3.3 Firebase Crashlytics

Provided by Google LLC. Automatically collects information about crashes and errors, including:

  • Device state at the time of the crash
  • Operating system version
  • A randomly generated installation identifier

This information helps us diagnose and fix bugs. It does not include your personal identity.

3.4 Google AdMob

Provided by Google LLC. Used to display advertisements within the App. AdMob may collect:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple's IDFA)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • Device type, operating system version, and app version
  • Limited interaction data with advertisements (views and clicks)

You can reset or limit ad tracking at any time through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → Reset advertising ID / Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → toggle off "Allow Apps to Request to Track"

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

3.5 Referral System

The App includes a referral feature that issues each user a randomly generated nine-digit code. To make this work, our backend stores:

  • The randomly generated nine-digit referral code
  • A Firebase Installation ID associated with your installation
  • A count of successful referrals
  • The reward state (whether ad-free access is active and when it expires)

The referral system does not store your name, email address, phone number, contact list, location, or any other personally identifying information. If you uninstall the App, the Installation ID is regenerated when you reinstall, and any unredeemed rewards will not carry over.
